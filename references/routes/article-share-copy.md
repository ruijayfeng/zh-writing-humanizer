# Article Share Copy Route

Use this route only when a finalized or near-final public article is available
and the user wants short copy for sharing, forwarding, Moments, a channel post,
or an article preview.

## Read The Article Before Writing

Extract four things from the finished article:

1. the strongest concrete result, conflict, or discovery;
2. why that result matters to the intended reader;
3. one unresolved limit or surprise, when it helps create honest curiosity;
4. the destination and expected length.

Do not summarize every section. Share copy opens one door into the article.

## Build The Copy

Prefer this movement:

- begin with the most interesting supplied result, problem, or reaction;
- add just enough context to make it understandable;
- state what the article actually contains;
- end with a natural invitation when the destination needs one.

The copy may sound excited, amused, frustrated, or surprised when the article
contains the event that caused that reaction. Preserve the author's energy,
but do not borrow another creator's greeting, catchphrase, emoji rhythm, or
engagement formula.

## Keep It Honest

- Do not invent a hands-on result, metric, conversation, or reader response.
- Do not turn a bounded test into a universal product verdict.
- Do not promise a tutorial, source code, template, or download that the
  article does not provide.
- Do not paste the article opening and call it share copy.
- Do not stack vague praise such as `太强了`, `封神`, or `颠覆` without naming
  what happened.

## Platform Variants

- **WeChat preview or forwarding copy:** one compact paragraph, usually two to
  four sentences; make the reading payoff explicit.
- **Moments:** more personal and lighter; one concrete reaction plus the useful
  reason to read is enough.
- **Zhihu or community introduction:** state the question or judgment earlier
  and reduce suspense or engagement language.

When the user does not name a platform, return one general share caption and
label the assumed use. Do not manufacture multiple variants merely to make the
answer look complete.

## Quality Gate

Before returning the copy, check:

- every claim can be located in the article;
- the opening contains a concrete noun, event, or judgment;
- a reader can tell why the article is worth opening;
- the copy does not repeat the title in different words;
- the tone carries human reaction without becoming a performance;
- no creator-specific verbal signature has been imitated.
