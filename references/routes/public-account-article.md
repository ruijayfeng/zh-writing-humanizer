# Public-Account Article Route

Use this route for WeChat public-account long-form articles, public-facing AI or
technology writing, product experiences, tool workflows, project stories,
trend commentary, and requested Zhihu adaptations. Do not use it for a neutral
API reference, short social post, press release, or a technical explanation
whose main value is reconstructing a mechanism.

## Resolve The Platform And Promise

Before drafting, identify:

- the destination: WeChat public account or Zhihu;
- the reader who should care;
- the one useful promise the article can honestly keep;
- which facts, tests, experiences, screenshots, numbers, and opinions are
  supported by the source;
- which important evidence is missing.

Do not compensate for missing material with invented first-person scenes. If a
product has not been tested, write an analysis of available evidence rather
than a hands-on review. If the requested premise outruns the evidence, narrow
the promise.

## Check Whether The Topic Can Carry An Article

A viable public article usually has at least two of these qualities:

- **Curiosity:** a concrete result, tension, surprise, or unanswered question;
- **Usefulness:** information, a method, a decision aid, or a reusable model;
- **Recognition:** a real frustration, desire, tradeoff, or experience readers
  can recognize without being told how to feel.

When only one quality is present, improve the angle using existing material or
state what additional evidence would be needed. Do not create a fictional
conflict for a weak topic.

## Choose A Shape, Not A Template

Select the lightest structure that fits the material:

- **Experience:** motivation or task → actual use → result → friction → judgment;
- **Tool or tutorial:** desired outcome → minimum setup → key decisions and
  steps → verification → cost, limits, and who should use it;
- **Phenomenon or opinion:** observable event → why it matters → evidence and
  mechanism → alternative reading → bounded conclusion;
- **Project retrospective:** initial goal → decisions → obstacles → outcome →
  what changed in the writer's understanding;
- **Update or trend:** what changed → difference from the previous state →
  practical consequence → what remains uncertain.

These are reasoning shapes. Merge, reorder, or skip moves that do not serve the
article.

For product experience, testing, workflow, or project material, choose the
shape from the events rather than assigning notes to feature headings. Read
`experience-narrative.md`. Before drafting, identify which cases establish a
baseline, increase difficulty, transfer the idea, challenge the first reading,
or close the question. Not every role needs a case, and sections do not need to
be equal length.

A structure has failed when several sections merely restate the same standard,
when the first observable result arrives after a long block of product
description, or when the ending repeats the opening judgment without anything
having changed or narrowed it.

An event-led structure can also fail by becoming too complete. If every section
contains a clean setup, surprise, explanation, and lesson, the article reads as
retrospective choreography. Preserve irregular source texture, leave some
results uninterpreted, and do not make every defect serve one thesis.

## Enter Quickly

Open with one of the following when the source supports it:

- a concrete result or before-and-after difference;
- a specific task, frustration, or moment of discovery;
- a surprising but verifiable fact;
- a clear judgment whose evidence follows immediately;
- a question created by an actual observation.

Do not begin with industry-scale importance, a history lesson, an empty
greeting, or a promise to provide a "deep dive." A greeting may remain when it
is part of an established supplied sample, but it must not delay the subject.

## Keep The Article Moving

Each section should do at least one real job: advance the experience, reveal
evidence, explain a mechanism, change the reader's judgment, or establish a
limit. Background material belongs near the point where it becomes necessary.

For product and tool articles:

- show what was attempted before describing every feature;
- interpret screenshots, outputs, and steps rather than using them as filler;
- separate observed behavior from product claims;
- name the setup, version, price, availability, or test condition when it
  changes the conclusion;
- include meaningful friction and failure, not ceremonial "pros and cons";
- end with who should try it, who can skip it, or what remains unproven.

When the material supports it, let the writer's next action grow from the
result just shown: a success can invite a harder test; a defect can trigger a
repair, workaround, or narrower claim; an irrelevant input can be discarded.
This local movement is more useful than adding a generic transition between
feature sections.

For tutorials, give the reader an observable checkpoint after consequential
steps. Do not call a tutorial "zero threshold" or "foolproof" when it requires
accounts, paid plans, technical setup, region access, or troubleshooting.

## Use Public Writing Techniques Carefully

- A title may promise a result, expose a tension, name a surprising object, or
  state a judgment, but every promise must be paid off in the article.
- A short paragraph can create emphasis; repeated fragments cannot replace
  reasoning.
- A personal reaction can reveal judgment; it cannot substitute for evidence.
- Prefer supplied wording and behavior to generic reaction labels. Do not add
  `我差点以为`, `有点惊喜`, `有点好笑`, or `这让我意识到` merely to simulate
  presence.
- Excitement, disappointment, surprise, irritation, and humor are welcome when
  a supplied event gives them a cause. Do not flatten public writing into a
  permanently calm or restrained persona.
- A callback to the opening can create closure when the article has genuinely
  changed the reader's understanding.
- A broader reflection belongs only when it grows directly from the case. End
  on the last useful claim when no larger conclusion is earned.

## Adapt For Zhihu

When the destination is Zhihu, keep the same public writing voice but change the
delivery:

- answer the stated question or give the main judgment near the beginning;
- reduce feed-style suspense, greetings, and calls for engagement;
- make evidence, assumptions, and counterexamples easier to inspect;
- use descriptive headings when they help long-term lookup;
- address a real competing interpretation only when the material supports one;
- close by resolving the question, not by asking for follows or likes.

A WeChat draft should not be pasted into Zhihu unchanged. Preserve facts and
judgment, then rebuild the entry, section order, and ending.

## Output And Gate

Unless the user asks for an audit, return the finished article. Before returning
it, verify that:

- the title and opening make a promise the body actually keeps;
- first-person claims come from supplied evidence;
- the article contains a visible judgment, not a feature catalog;
- technical explanations remain accurate after simplification;
- costs, limitations, failures, and uncertainty remain visible;
- paragraph rhythm is readable on a phone without becoming theatrical;
- no recognizable creator catchphrases or copied signature structure appear;
- a requested byline uses `凯冰`, and no legal name appears;
- the base humanizer quality gate still passes.
