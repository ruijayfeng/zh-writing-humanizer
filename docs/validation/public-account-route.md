# Public-Account Writing Route Fixture

## Input

Draft a Chinese WeChat public-account article from supplied notes about using a
coding agent to organize an existing knowledge base. The notes include the
actual workflow, two failed attempts, the final folder structure, and a rough
personal judgment. They do not include time-saved metrics, reader reactions,
conversations, or a dramatic origin story. Use the public-account writing style
and add a byline.

## Expected AI Traces

- A generic AI-industry opening would delay the real workflow.
- A feature catalog would hide the writer's decisions and failed attempts.
- Invented time savings, dialogue, reader feedback, or emotional scenes would
  violate the first-person evidence boundary.
- Borrowed creator catchphrases, exaggerated punctuation, or a fixed viral
  closing would turn platform fit into imitation.
- Using a legal name in the byline would violate the identity rule.

## Acceptable Rewrite

The article begins with the resulting knowledge-base change, the problem that
made the change useful, or one supplied failure. It walks through the actual
workflow while interpreting the consequential decisions instead of listing
every click. The two failures remain visible and inform the final judgment. It
does not invent efficiency numbers, conversations, screenshots, or reactions.
The ending states who may benefit, what the workflow still does not solve, or
what the writer would change next. The byline is `凯冰`.

For a Zhihu adaptation, the answer states its main judgment near the beginning,
uses descriptive headings where useful, makes assumptions and limitations easy
to inspect, and removes feed-style calls for engagement.

## Gate

- The public-account and technical-article routes produce meaningfully
  different openings, pacing, and endings.
- Personal warmth comes from supplied experience and judgment, not invention.
- The article pays off its title and opening without hype.
- A requested byline uses `凯冰`; no legal name appears.
- The Zhihu adaptation changes platform delivery without changing facts.
